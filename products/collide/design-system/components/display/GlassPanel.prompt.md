The base container for every pane, card, popover and composer. Pair with `PanelHeader` for mono-labelled sections.
```jsx
<GlassPanel radius="xl"><PanelHeader title="Callouts" />…</GlassPanel>
<GlassPanel variant="floating" glow padding={16}>…</GlassPanel>
```
- `variant`: solid | floating (backdrop blur). `glow`: signal edge. `pattern`: dot/hatch/grid texture layer.
