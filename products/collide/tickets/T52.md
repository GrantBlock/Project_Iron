**Goal:** The Paper swatch, and "Match system" on a light computer, use the Aura **paper** ground colours instead of the old Collide Light theme.

**Source:** Grant, 2026-11-20: "update the light theme to use paper theme colors, I preferred the paper colors over the previous light collide."

**Files in scope:** `app/src/shell/prefs.ts`, `Sidebar.tsx`, `shell.css`, `aura.css` (only if a paper tweak is needed), `app/src/App.test.tsx`, `prefs.test.ts`, tests.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- Choosing the Paper swatch puts the Aura paper ground on the whole app (`data-app-theme="aura"`, `data-aura-theme="paper"`), so the app looks as the Paper swatch does, with the paper accent (amber) and the paper tile and surface colours.

- Anything that branches on the theme (splash banner dots, Recents labels, tab and tag styles written for `aura`) works with Paper like any other Aura ground; check the splash banner and the Recents day labels in Paper.
