Draft (not yet approved by Grant)

  1. Chat is home. Recents on the left, the open chat on the right, like Agent Chat.
  2. Snip and Editor have no page of their own. They open as a workspace when you open a snip, a zip file or an agent's files.
  3. Workspace = header (name, save state, Save, Close) + toolbar + canvas + numbered list. Chat moves to a dock on the right. Close returns to chat; unsaved changes ask first.
  4. Zips appear in Recents and expand to their folder tree.
  5. Agents open their Personality and Memory files, and their picture.
  6. Collapse (chat header, or the workspace header) shrinks the whole window to Recents and Chat side by side. An open snip or file stays open and shows in the sidebar with Expand.
  7. Themes: System, Light, Dark, Aura (14 grounds). The banner redraws from the theme. The logo is never recoloured.
